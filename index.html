About Me

I received my Master's degree in Applied Mathematics from Université Paris Cité, Paris, France, in 2022. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. at the LTCI laboratory of Télécom Paris, Palaiseau, France, under the joint supervision of Florence Tupin, Christophe Kervazo, and Loïc Denis.

My research focuses on developing deep learning techniques for processing and analyzing Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data, specifically addressing spatial speckle correlation and tomographic reconstruction.
